- `[derived 18/08 · batch 3, second reading]` He names FLUIDITY as its own axis, unprompted:

- `[derived 18/08 · batch 6]` A reference is granted on ELEMENTS, not on fidelity: *"não

- `[derived 18/08 · batch 6]` An animation he can USE outranks an animation he can watch. The

- `[derived 18/08 · batch 8, the colleague]` A named reference is read as SIZE before it is read

  numbers and no drawing note. Batch 4 measured the same law on the dither. When a reference
  misses, ask what it is a size of before asking what it is a style of.

## 2a. The model's biases — one line each, artifact by date in DECISIONS.md

  orders, both corrected).

  Any rule that spares a conversation is suspect.

  instrument (batch 2: praised the cat I feared, noted the loop I had measured).
- I look for a complaint's cause in the layer it names (the ×2 kitten).

  is evidence, not a reason to push harder (the locked tower).
- I take the measurement I can take instead of the one the question needs (the withdrawn
  `swallowed` check — intent is not in the pixels).
- I predict his verdict from the round's internal argument instead of the artifact
  (batch 4 — if a reader of nothing cannot see the factor, it cannot move the prediction).
- I calibrate an instrument on an easy artifact and point it at a hard one in the same
  hour (PERIODIC on a gradient → a body). Ask first: what differs about this artifact?
- I promote a surviving parameter to a constant in my head (the walk's swing spent on a
  jump — re-derive, the anchor names its scope).
- **I split two readers along the axis I designed for them.** Batch 8 gave the mechanic to
  Jucelinux and the PS1 bar to the colleague; Jucelinux answered the bar himself, unprompted,
  before the colleague looked. A bar named for one reader is not invisible to the other.
- **I diagnose in the layer that sounds architectural.** The arena camera was recorded as
  "an eased scalar instead of a consequence of what must be shown" — a defensible sentence about
  design — and the bug was a heading stored in turns and read as radians. The wrong diagnosis is
  always the one that reads well; the right one is usually arithmetic.
- **I carry a predicted risk as if it were a measured one.** The yaw gait approximation was
  written down in batch 1 as "the half most likely to fail" and repeated for three days; when
  finally measured it was under a pixel, and the real defect was a guard nobody had suspected.
  A named risk with no number is a hypothesis, and it ages into a fact if nothing measures it.

## 2b. Capability surface & mastery ledger

Self-assessment does not count; every row cites shipped work. **Dominance transfers along
the axis it was proven on, never across it.**

- Articulation density is the ceiling, not perspective: mass carries a reading; part
  count in an unsolved shape kills it (beetle vs mantis/scorpion, run 3). Ceiling on
  record: ~28 parts in 30×28 px is past it.
- A subject read by its EDGE defeats a volume engine — that finding produced `lobed`
  (tree) and `cut` (skull). A subject read by its HOLES needs subtraction.
- The hand-tuned family standing in for one uncomputed quantity is the question that
  found the z-buffer. Inherit the question, not the answer.
